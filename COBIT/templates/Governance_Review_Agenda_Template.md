This is a governing-body-level review (EDM domain) — distinct from an operational management
review. The audience is the board or audit committee; the purpose is to evaluate direction given
to management and hold management to account for outcomes, not to review day-to-day operations.

## 1. Status of actions from the previous governance review
- [ ] Review outstanding decisions/actions from the prior meeting; confirm closure status.

## 2. Design factor changes since last review (EDM01)
- Enterprise strategy changes
- Changes in risk profile or risk appetite/tolerance
- New or changed I&T-related issues
- Regulatory or threat landscape changes
- Sourcing model changes (e.g., new outsourcing, cloud migration)

## 3. Benefits delivery (EDM02)
- Summary of IT investment value realization vs. business case
- Portfolio-level status: programs/projects delivering expected benefits vs. those at risk

## 4. Risk optimization (EDM03)
- [ ] Current enterprise IT-related risk profile vs. risk appetite
- [ ] Material new/closed risks since last review
- [ ] Status of risk treatment plans for high-priority risks

## 5. Resource optimization (EDM04)
- Adequacy of IT capabilities (people, process, technology) against demand
- Resourcing gaps flagged by management

## 6. Stakeholder engagement and reporting (EDM05)
- Feedback from key stakeholders (customers, regulators, auditors, business units)
- Assessment of whether management reporting to this body has been transparent and accurate

## 7. Capability assessment summary
- Current vs. target capability levels for prioritized governance/management objectives
  (reference `COBIT_Capability_Assessment_Matrix_Template.csv`)
- Objectives showing the largest capability gaps and proposed remediation sequencing

## 8. Assurance and compliance summary (MEA02-MEA04)
- Internal control environment status and any material control deficiencies
- Compliance status against applicable external requirements
- Summary of independent assurance reviews completed or planned
